What this proves

And, just as usefully, what it does not.

What this is

  • Evidence that I write and ship working software, rather than brief someone who does.
  • A read on how I think: find the friction, build the smallest thing that removes it, put it somewhere people can use it.
  • Enough fluency to tell an engineering team what I need, and to know when the answer I am getting is wrong.

What this is not

  • Production infrastructure at studio scale. These are personal-scale tools with real users, not enterprise pipelines.
  • A claim to be an ML engineer. I am a creative who builds, and the translation between those two rooms is the actual skill.
  • Finished. Most of these are still moving, which is rather the point of a slate.
